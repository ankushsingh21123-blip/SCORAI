## Slide 5: The Tri-Core Architecture (Unrivaled Tech Stack)
### 1. Jarvis & Shannon (The Orchestrator & The Stress Battery)
- **Jarvis:** The autonomous brain. Point it at an endpoint, and it maps the attack surface via headless Chromium (Playwright) and API parsing.
- **Shannon AI:** The ammunition. A high-velocity stress battery firing 2,000+ adversarial probes (Prompt Injection, Data Exfiltration, Semantic Drift) to shatter weak guardrails.

### 2. S.O.V.A (Stochastic Optimization for Vulnerability Analysis)
- The elite red-teaming engine designed to bypass RLHF. Using **Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)**, S.O.V.A plays a continuous, non-deterministic "game" against the target AI. 
- It builds deep context over multi-turn interactions, navigating the target's "logic maze" until it forces a critical failure.

### 3. MEL (Modified Expected Loss Actuarial Model)
- The financial translator. Calculates the statistical probability of a semantic failure and maps it to concrete financial liabilities (fines, brand damage, legal costs).
- **Output:** An annualized financial exposure metric (e.g., $2.4M USD PML) and the definitive **SRS Score**.
